Improving Mental Health Through Gut Wellness:
The Hidden Power of Microbes and Probiotics
Roshd Educational Magazine – Published: June 2025
Original Persian title:
«گوارش تا سلامت روان – نقش شگفت‌انگیز میکروب‌ها و پروبیوتیک‌ها»
English Summary:
This article explores the emerging scientific link between gut microbiota and mental health, explaining how microbial balance, probiotics, and diet influence mood regulation, cognitive performance, and stress response. It summarizes recent research on gut–brain communication and highlights how microbial interventions may support psychological well-being. Written for a nationwide audience, the article translates complex biological concepts into clear scientific messages for students and educators.
