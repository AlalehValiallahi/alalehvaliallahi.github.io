A Day When Drugs May No Longer Work:
The Global Crisis of Antimicrobial Resistance

Published: 2026

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is transforming once-treatable infections into a growing global health threat. This article explores how excessive and inappropriate antibiotic use accelerates bacterial adaptation, allowing resistant microorganisms to survive and spread. By presenting global antibiotic consumption patterns and explaining the consequences of antimicrobial pressure, the article highlights why antibiotic effectiveness cannot be taken for granted and why responsible antimicrobial use is essential for protecting future generations.

A future where common infections become difficult or impossible to treat is no longer a distant concern. Antimicrobial resistance has become one of the defining challenges of modern medicine.
