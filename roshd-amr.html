A Day When Drugs May No Longer Work:
The Global Crisis of Antimicrobial Resistance

Published: 2026

Antimicrobial resistance is transforming once-treatable infections into a growing global health threat. This article explores how excessive and inappropriate antibiotic use accelerates bacterial adaptation, allowing resistant microorganisms to survive and spread. Through global antibiotic consumption patterns and scientific explanations, the article highlights why antibiotic effectiveness cannot be taken for granted and why responsible antimicrobial use is essential for protecting future generations.

A future where common infections become untreatable is no longer a distant possibility — antimicrobial resistance has become one of the defining challenges of modern medicine.
